Future Before/After photo uploads
=================================

Use this folder for job card photos.

Bulk originals (optional):
- Put copies of your source files in uploads/ — see uploads/README.txt

Recommended naming:
- before-<job-name>-01.jpg
- after-<job-name>-01.jpg

Current default images used by the app:
- before-default.png
- after-default.png

Tip:
When adding a new job in CRM, upload your new photos in the "Before photos" and "After photos" fields.
