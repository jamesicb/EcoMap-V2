Upload here (optional file archive)
====================================

Drop or copy your original before/after images into this folder so they stay
with the project (backups, hand-off to a designer, etc.).

This folder is not read automatically by the app. To show photos on job cards:

1. Open Jobs gallery → Add job (or Edit).
2. Use "Before photos" and "After photos" to pick the files (from this folder or anywhere on your PC).

Naming suggestion:
- before-<address-or-job-id>-01.jpg
- after-<address-or-job-id>-01.jpg

Default images for jobs with no photos yet live one level up:
- ../before-default.png
- ../after-default.png
