Privacy notice

Who we are. Absolute Acrylics (Warmliving Ireland Ltd) is the data controller for enquiries submitted through this site.

What we collect. Quote form: name, email, phone, and optional message. Map “locate me” (only if you tap the button): your device location stays on your device and is not sent to our servers. When Google Analytics is configured, we also send anonymous events for landing-page views, exploring nearby projects, and completed quote enquiries. We do not send your name, email, phone, or message to analytics.

Why we use it. To respond to your enquiry and provide a quotation (legitimate interest / pre-contract steps). Anonymous usage events are used only to understand how many visitors explore nearby projects and complete an enquiry.

How long we keep it. Enquiry records are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and our business records, then deleted or anonymised.

Your rights. You may request access, correction, or erasure of your personal data, or object to processing, by contacting info@absoluteacrylics.ie. You may lodge a complaint with the Irish Data Protection Commission.
